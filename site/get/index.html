The Discerner

Get the Android app.

Download the app
  1. Open the downloaded file (Chrome shows it at the bottom, or look in Downloads).
  2. If asked, allow Chrome to install apps, then tap Install.
  3. If Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap "Install anyway". It's a private app, not from the Play Store.

On iPhone, add the Discerner to your Home Screen.

Open the Discerner
  1. Open it in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open it from the Discerner icon on your Home Screen.
